The system

Where the machine comes in and where the specialists do

Automation does not replace judgement. It makes judgement apply every day instead of once a month, whenever someone finds a gap to review the account.

01 Capturing the right signals +

Conversion events, CRM and closed sales in one place. If what you measure is not a real sale, everything downstream optimises toward the wrong number.

Automated: collection and cleanup. Human: defining what counts as a sale.

02 Creative production +

Ad variants generated and adapted with AI from the angles that already work, in cycles of days rather than weeks. Creative is what moves cost per customer the most.

Automated: versions and formats. Human: the angle and the final approval.

03 Understanding the platform +

Every platform has an algorithm that learns from what you feed it. Knowing which signals it rewards, how long it takes to exit learning and how much you can move it defines account structure, budget and the pace of changes.

Automated: budget rules and pausing. Human: the structure, the limits and the exceptions.

A live dashboard with cost per customer, attributed revenue and the status of every campaign, open whenever you want it. No waiting for the month-end report.

Automated: the dashboard. Human: reading it and deciding what to do.

What we measure

Results that get measured and scaled.

Volume

How many leads or sales come in each month. It is the first number to move, but on its own it says nothing: growing volume without controlling cost just makes you spend faster.

Cost per acquisition

What each new customer costs you. It is the target we set in week one and the basis for deciding which campaign scales and which one gets cut.

ROAS

How much comes back for every unit invested. It is the number that connects ad spend to your books and the one you take to the board.

FAQ - Frequently asked questions

Will AI write my ads with nobody reviewing them?

No. AI produces variants and speeds up production; a strategist defines the angle and approves everything that goes live. Nothing is published without human review.

Is there a contract?

Yes. We ask for three months so campaigns gather enough data. After that the relationship runs month to month.

How long until I see results?

The first leads usually arrive within the first few weeks. Bringing cost per customer down in a stable way takes two to three months of testing.

Who owns the accounts and the automations?

You do. We work inside your accounts with partner access, and if you ever stop working with us, you keep the history and the workflows we built.

Do you work outside my city?

Yes, across Mexico and remotely elsewhere. Meetings are by video call and the dashboard is open whenever you want it.
