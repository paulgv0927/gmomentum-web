6. Your ARCO rights

You have the right to know what personal data we hold about you and what we use it for (Access), to ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information (Rectification), to request deletion where you consider it is not required for any of the stated purposes (Cancellation), and to object to the use of your data for specific purposes (Objection).

To exercise any of these rights, email the address in section 1 with:

  • Your name and a means of replying to you
  • A copy of official identification proving who you are

We will respond within a maximum of twenty business days from receipt. Exercising these rights is free of charge.
