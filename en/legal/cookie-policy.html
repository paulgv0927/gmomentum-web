Cookie policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This policy explains what cookies are, which ones this site uses and how you can control them.

1. What they are

A cookie is a small file a website stores in your browser. It remembers information between visits: from your decision about this very notice to data that makes it possible to count how many people visited the page.

2. Which cookies we use

CategoryWhat it doesNeeds your permission?
Necessary Remembers whether you accepted or declined cookies, so we do not ask again on every visit. No
Measurement Tells us how many people visit, where they come from and which sections they read. We use [GOOGLE ANALYTICS 4]. Yes
Advertising Measures which campaigns bring visits and shows ads to people who already visited. We use [META PIXEL]. Yes

Measurement and advertising cookies do not activate until you accept them. If you decline, the site works exactly the same: you lose no functionality.

3. How long they last

The cookie storing your decision about this notice stays until you delete it or change your choice. Measurement and advertising cookies last for the periods set by their respective providers, usually between a few days and twenty-four months.

4. How to change your decision

You can change your choice at any time through the Cookie settings link in the footer of any page. You can also block or delete cookies from your browser's privacy settings. Note that blocking cookies in the browser affects every site you visit, not just this one.

5. Personal data

Processing of any data collected through cookies is governed by our privacy notice.

6. Changes

If we add or remove measurement tools, we will update this page and the date shown above.
